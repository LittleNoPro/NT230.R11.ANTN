\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
The reconstructed graph detector achieves high F1 on the random and grouped splits. Logistic regression
on TF-IDF features achieves higher F1 on the grouped splits and higher recall and lower FPR on held-out
sources. Adding benign training files lowers GAT's pooled false-positive rate but also lowers recall,
including on the small set of webshells from held-out sources.

The fixture study shows that the graph representation can discard variable dependencies, exception paths
and namespace information. Evaluating deployment would require independently reviewed labels, more diverse
software projects and thresholds calibrated on separate validation data.
